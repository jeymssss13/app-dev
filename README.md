# My Favorite Movie: Demon Slayer – Infinity Castle Arc

---

## Overview
**Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Arc** (Kimetsu no Yaiba: Mugen Shiro-hen) is the *upcoming finale* of the **Demon Slayer** anime series, based on the manga by *Koyoharu Gotouge*.  
It follows the epic conclusion of the battle between the **Demon Slayer Corps** and **Muzan Kibutsuji**, the first and most powerful demon.

---

## Main Characters
- **Tanjiro Kamado** – A kind-hearted swordsman determined to save his sister  
- **Nezuko Kamado** – Tanjiro’s demon sister who fights to regain her humanity  
- **Zenitsu Agatsuma** – A cowardly yet lightning-fast fighter  
- **Inosuke Hashibira** – A wild warrior with beast-like instincts 
- **Muzan Kibutsuji** – The main antagonist and creator of all demons  

---

## Plot Summary
> “No matter how many times life knocks you down, stand up and fight again.” – Tanjiro Kamado  

After the *Swordsmith Village Arc*, the Demon Slayer Corps infiltrates Muzan’s **Infinity Castle**, a twisted dimension filled with powerful demons.  
Each Hashira faces deadly opponents as Tanjiro and his friends struggle to end Muzan’s reign once and for all.

---

## Favorite Moments
- 🌀 **Opening scene** – When Tanjiro and the others are falling through the Infinity Castle  
- ⚡ **Zenitsu vs. Kaigaku** – A powerful and emotional battle between former Thunder Breathing users  
- 🔥 **Tanjiro and Giyu vs. Akaza** – One of the most intense and beautifully animated fights in the entire series
